# Visual direction and image provenance

The public [Sterling Brokers website](https://www.sterlingbrokers.com/) was reviewed on October 16, 2026 for visual reference: slate navy, lime, serif headlines and generous whitespace. This independent case does not reproduce a Sterling platform interface or imply affiliation.

## Original image

- Method: built-in ImageGen, original conceptual artwork.
- Saved web asset: [implementation-paths.jpg](../assets/implementation-paths.jpg).
- The generated PNG was converted to JPEG for delivery without changing its composition.
- Role: a visual metaphor for coordinating dependencies before go-live. The actual findings and decision are rendered separately from the committed generated report.

### Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original editorial hero artwork for an independent employee benefits implementation portfolio case. Premium architectural still life in landscape 3:2. Four broad matte ivory paper-ceramic ribbons or pathways converge across a warm limestone surface toward one precise freestanding rectangular slate-blue threshold. Small restrained lime-green checkpoint blocks sit beside each path. One ribbon stops just short of an unresolved join, suggesting a dependency that must be confirmed before moving forward. Calm order and coordination, sophisticated tactile paper and ceramic materials, soft natural window daylight, refined long shadows, quiet editorial photography with physically convincing 3D forms. Muted dark slate #1b2c3b, warm ivory #f3f1e8, fresh lime #d1ff7e. Composition weighted to the right with uncluttered negative space on the left, clear broad forms that stay readable at small sizes. Conceptual metaphor, not a real system or an insurance approval. No text, numbers, letters, logos, UI, screens, people, shields, badges, arrows, checkmark confetti or watermark. Opaque background.

## Other assets

The portrait was supplied by Felipe Mattos. DM Serif Display and Inter are open font alternatives, self-hosted from their official Google Fonts distributions, with licenses in `assets/fonts/`. They are not presented as Sterling's proprietary fonts.

## Evidence boundaries

All records, plan rules and decisions are fictional. The workbook is a static coordination snapshot; editing it does not rerun the audit. Passing the example checks does not authorize launch or create insurance coverage. No customer outcomes or time savings were measured.
